Best on your phone. Scan to open.

Point your phone's camera at the code. It opens HazeNow, ready to add to your Home Screen.

  1. The full app on your Home Screen. Three taps from Safari.

    Open HazeNow in Safari

    1. Open HazeNow in Safari.
    2. Tap Share. On iOS 26, tap ••• first.
    3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
    4. For alerts, open it from your Home Screen and tap Get alerts. iOS 16.4 or newer, Singapore only for now.

    Scriptable is a free app by another developer. Our script is open source.

    1. Install Scriptable from the App Store.
    2. Tap Add the HazeNow widget, then Download, and open the file in Scriptable.
    3. Long-press your Home Screen, tap + (or Edit, Add Widget) and add a Scriptable widget.
    4. Long-press it, tap Edit Widget and set Script to HazeNow. Type an area like Tampines in Parameter to show it.

    Add the HazeNow widget Get Scriptable

    Lock Screen: tap Customize, add a Scriptable widget and set it the same way.

    1. Tap Copy script, or open the script and copy it.
    2. In Scriptable, tap +, paste, name it HazeNow and tap Done.

  2. Install from Chrome. Three taps, no warnings.

    Open HazeNow in Chrome

    1. Open HazeNow in Chrome.
    2. Tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
    3. Tap Install.
    4. For alerts, open it and tap Get alerts. Singapore only for now.

    Widgets, a Quick Settings tile and calm band alerts. Android 8 or newer.

    Download for Android HazeNow-android.apk

    It's not on the Play Store yet, so Android checks with you once. That's expected.

    1. Open the downloaded file.
    2. If asked, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source.
    3. Tap Install. If Play Protect doesn't recognise it, tap More details, then Install anyway. Samsung or Xiaomi: Install anyway or Continue.

    Obtainium (free, open source) installs updates straight from our GitHub releases. Get it from GitHub or F-Droid, then add https://github.com/%RELEASES_REPO%.

  3. ● 105 ▲ in your menu bar, and a pill by the notch. macOS 14 or newer. Beta

    Download for Mac HazeNow-mac.zip

    macOS asks once, because we don't pay Apple US$99 a year to verify it. The code is open.

    1. Unzip, drag HazeNow into Applications and open it.
    2. macOS says it could not verify HazeNow. Click Done.
    3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway next to “HazeNow” was blocked.
    4. Click Open Anyway again and enter your password. HazeNow is now in your menu bar.

    No warning at all: the SwiftBar plugin
